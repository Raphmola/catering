About Georgias Catering Services Ltd

A wholly Nigerian-owned service provider built on reliability, hygiene, and industrial scale.

GEORGIAS CATERING SERVICES LTD is a fully Nigerian-owned company that leads with efficiency in the field of hospitality in the oil and gas industry. We understand the rigorous demands of offshore installations, marine vessels, drilling rigs, and industrial corporate environments.

Our commitment goes beyond conventional food supply. We integrate world-class catering standards with comprehensive facility management, accommodation upkeep, and round-the-clock emergency support to ensure your workforce remains healthy, comfortable, and productive.

Our Core Standards

  • Appreciable Portions: Generous, well-balanced meal portions tailored for high-energy workforce requirements.
  • Logistics Readiness: Experienced in handling marine logistics, containerized provisions, and offshore transfers.
